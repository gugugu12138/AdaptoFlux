Native GraphEvo type-prior replication: completed 90/90 formal runs

Design
30 fixed formal seeds 110600..110629; pilot seed 110500 excluded. Nine original operator bodies unchanged. Fine: pos_type,target_type; coarse: quantity,quantity (distinct from scalar); flat: scalar,scalar. Every callback exhaustively evaluates all 20 unequal positions/targets in {-2,-1,0,1,2}, at most four graph executions per episode. Every evaluation asserts unchanged Python/NumPy RNG states. No evaluation caching.

Primary results (strict success on all 20 states)

fine | 29/30 | 83.3%-99.4% | 0.9667 | 0.0667 | 53.03 | 6.43 | 6.87
coarse | 15/30 | 33.2%-66.8% | 0.5033 | 0.9900 | 73.23 | 8.43 | 5.60

The legacy >=95% threshold yields the same run-success counts. Initialization alone succeeds in 20/30 fine, 4/30 coarse, and 0/30 flat runs. Final full-search results therefore must not be described as entirely initialization-only.

Interpretation
Task-semantic type declarations strongly reduce the candidate space and improve success in this controlled tiny environment. Merging position/target types degrades success, showing that directional role information contributes beyond a generic numeric-type distinction. This is evidence for a supplied type prior and routing mechanism, not proof of universal search superiority or a uniquely stateful semantics.

Audit caveats
GraphEvoTrainer.train breaks for early stopping before adding that cycle's steps_taken to total_refinement_steps and before updating the best snapshot. Hence result.json accepted_refinements is the raw native counter, incomplete on success; model/final plus final reevaluation is authoritative, not model/best.
Native random_single calls accuracy once for each initial model (3), once at each refinement phase start (cycles_completed), once per accepted substitution, and twice at final/best reporting. Thus accepted replacements = accuracy_calls - 3 - cycles_completed - 2. This follows the inspected native call sites; callback totals are independently verified against evaluation_events.jsonl.
Other experiments ran concurrently; wall times are execution records and must not be used for comparative speed claims.
Nominal search caps matched; observed callback/unique graph budgets differ. Prefix success tables below are post-hoc descriptive summaries, not separately rerun equal-budget trials.
Ordered attributed graph signatures, excluding literal node names; not semantic equivalence or isomorphism classes. Node replacement may change insertion order and execution order.

Execution
Set PYTHONHASHSEED=0 and PYTHONDONTWRITEBYTECODE=1. Use python -B run_type_search.py prepare, pilot, freeze, formal in sequence. The runner refuses to overwrite a completed stage. All writes are confined to this new type_search directory; ATF core and ATF-nodes were not modified.
